BeautyPlanner – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

In short: BeautyPlanner keeps your beauty journal on your own phone. We have no servers, no user accounts, no advertising and no analytics. We cannot see your data.

1. Who we are

BeautyPlanner (the "app") is an Android app for planning and recording beauty visits. The developer is Nexors Studio. Contact: nexors.studio@gmail.com.

2. Data you enter in the app

You can enter visits (date, time, salon, specialist, service, price, notes), saved salons, hair shade and formula notes, photos (visit photos, before/after photos, inspirations) and app settings. All of this is stored only on your device in the app's private storage. We do not receive, copy or have access to it. Because we hold no copy, we cannot look up, correct or delete it for you; you can do it yourself in the app, or delete everything by uninstalling the app.

3. What we do not do

  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no crash-reporting services.
  • No user accounts, sign-in or profiles.
  • No access to your location, contacts, microphone, or your whole photo library. Photos are added only when you pick them or take them with the system camera.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.

4. Permissions

  • Notifications, exact alarms, start after reboot, vibration: to remind you about upcoming visits at the right time, also after the phone restarts.
  • Billing / internet: to let Google Play handle the subscription.

5. Subscription and payments

The app offers a free period and an optional subscription handled entirely by Google Play. Google processes your payment; we never see your card or payment details. The app only receives from Google Play whether your subscription is active. Google's handling of your data is described in Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Android backup and device transfer

Android's built-in backup may store the date your free period started (a small file) in your Google account so that reinstalling the app does not restart the free period. When you set up a new phone, Android may also transfer the app's data from your old phone. This is done by Android/Google under your Google account settings, not by us.

7. Backup and export files you create

You can create a backup file (visits, settings and photos), a PDF report and a CSV file, and save or share them. These files contain personal data and photos and are not encrypted. Where you save or send them is your choice and responsibility; please keep them in a safe place.

8. Backup to your Google Drive (planned feature)

We are working on an optional automatic backup to your own Google Drive. It is not available in the app yet; this section describes how it will work so that you know in advance.

  • It is off by default and starts only after you give your consent in a Google dialog.
  • The app will use only Google's "application data folder" permission: a hidden folder in your Drive that only BeautyPlanner can access. The app cannot see or change your other Drive files.
  • The backup (visits, settings, photos) goes directly from your phone to your Drive. It does not pass through any server of ours, and we cannot read it.
  • You can turn it off at any time in the app, and revoke access or delete the backup in your Google Account (myaccount.google.com/permissions). Removing the app's access removes the hidden folder.
  • BeautyPlanner's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use this data only to provide the backup and restore feature, do not transfer it to anyone, and do not use it for advertising.

We will update this policy before the feature is released.

9. Retention and deletion

Data in the app stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Backup and export files you created, and (once available) a Drive backup, stay where you saved them until you delete them.

10. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU) and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

11. Your rights

Because we do not collect or store your personal data, we do not hold anything that we could provide, correct or erase. If you are in the EU/EEA/UK or California and have questions about your rights (access, rectification, erasure, portability, objection, complaint to a supervisory authority), contact us at the address above. We do not sell or share personal information (California CCPA/CPRA).

12. Health notice

Care tips and "Smart Insights" in the app are general information computed from your own entries. They are not medical advice.

13. Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version here with a new date.
