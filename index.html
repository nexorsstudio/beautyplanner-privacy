BeautyPlanner – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

In short: BeautyPlanner keeps your beauty journal on your own phone. We have no servers, no user accounts, no advertising and no analytics. We cannot see your data. If you choose to turn on the optional backup, your data goes only to your own Google Drive.

1. Who we are

BeautyPlanner (the "app") is an Android app for planning and recording beauty visits. The developer is Nexors Studio. Contact: nexors.studio@gmail.com.

2. Data you enter in the app

You can enter visits (date, time, salon, specialist, service, price, notes), saved salons, hair shade and formula notes, photos (visit photos, before/after photos, inspirations), a name, and app settings (for example language, currency, theme, categories and reminders). All of this is stored on your device in the app's private storage. We do not receive, copy or have access to it. Because we hold no copy, we cannot look up, correct or delete it for you; you can do it yourself in the app, or delete everything by uninstalling the app.

3. What we do not do

  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no crash-reporting services.
  • No user accounts, sign-in or profiles.
  • No access to your location, contacts, microphone, or your whole photo library. Photos are added only when you pick them with the system picker or take them with the system camera.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.

4. Permissions

  • Notifications, exact alarms, start after reboot, vibration: to remind you about upcoming visits at the exact time you chose, also after the phone restarts.
  • Internet: to let Google Play handle the subscription and, only if you turn it on, to back up to your Google Drive.

5. Subscription and payments

The app is free to use for a trial period of 50 days without a card. After that, the main features stay free and an optional subscription (BeautyPlanner Premium) unlocks additional features. Subscriptions may include a short free trial offered by Google Play. The subscription is handled entirely by Google Play. Google processes your payment; we never see your card or payment details. The app only receives from Google Play whether your subscription is active. You can manage or cancel it at any time in Google Play. Google's handling of your data is described in Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Android backup and device transfer

Android's built-in backup ("Backup by Google One") may store a copy of the app's text data in your Google account: your visits, notes, prices, saved salons, hair shades and formulas, categories, settings, and the date your free period started. It does not include your photos. If you reinstall the app or set up a new phone, Android can restore this data automatically. The date of the free period is stored so that reinstalling does not restart it. This is done by Android/Google under your Google account settings, not by us, and you can turn it off in your phone's settings (Google > Backup). When you set up a new phone, Android may also transfer all of the app's data, including photos, directly from your old phone.

7. Backup and export files you create

You can create a backup file (visits, settings and photos), a PDF report and a CSV file, and save or share them. These files contain personal data and photos and are not encrypted. Where you save or send them is your choice and responsibility; please keep them in a safe place, and restore only a backup file that you created yourself.

8. Backup to your Google Drive (optional)

BeautyPlanner can automatically back up your data to your own Google Drive. This is optional and off by default. It starts only after you turn it on in the app and give your consent once in a Google dialog.

  • What is backed up: your visits, notes, prices, saved salons, hair shades and formulas, inspirations, and settings (such as language, theme, categories, card order, reminders and the name you entered). If "Back up photos too" is on (it is on by default), also your photos: visit photos, before/after photos and inspiration images. Videos you export are not backed up.
  • Where it goes: to a hidden folder in your Drive (Google's "application data folder", permission drive.appdata) that only BeautyPlanner can access. The app cannot see or change your other Drive files. The data goes directly from your phone to your Drive over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). It does not pass through any server of ours, and we cannot read it.
  • When: about once a day on Wi-Fi when the battery is not low, and a few minutes after you change something. Only what changed is sent. You can also tap "Back up now".
  • Restore: on the first screen of a new phone or in Settings, "Restore from Google Drive" shows the date and size of the copy and asks for confirmation. A safety copy of the current data is kept so you can undo the restore.
  • Your control: you can turn the backup off at any time and choose to delete the copy from your Drive. If you turn off "Back up photos too", the photos already stored on your Drive are deleted. You can also revoke access or delete the data in your Google Account (myaccount.google.com/permissions).
  • BeautyPlanner's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use this data only to provide the backup and restore feature, do not transfer it to anyone, and do not use it for advertising.

Google handles the data stored in your Drive under its own terms and Privacy Policy.

9. Retention and deletion

Data in the app stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Backup and export files you created stay where you saved them until you delete them. A Google Drive backup stays in your Drive until you turn the backup off and delete the copy in the app, or delete it in your Google Account. The copy made by Android's automatic backup stays in your Google account until you delete it in your phone's backup settings or Google Account.

10. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU) and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

11. Your rights

Because we do not collect or store your personal data on any server of ours, we do not hold anything that we could provide, correct or erase. If you are in the EU/EEA/UK or California and have questions about your rights (access, rectification, erasure, portability, objection, complaint to a supervisory authority), contact us at the address above. We do not sell or share personal information (California CCPA/CPRA).

12. Health notice

Care tips and "Smart Insights" in the app are general information computed from your own entries. They are not medical advice.

13. Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version here with a new date.
